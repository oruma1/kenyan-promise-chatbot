AZIMIO LA UMOJA MANIFESTO 2022 - EXTRACT

HEALTHCARE
We will ensure that every Kenyan has access to affordable healthcare.
Our government shall implement universal health coverage within the first
100 days. We commit to building 50 new hospitals across all 47 counties.
We promise to hire 20,000 additional healthcare workers.

EDUCATION
We shall introduce free lunch programs in all public primary schools.
Our government will ensure that no child is sent home for lack of school fees.
We commit to increasing the education budget to 30 percent of the national budget.

ECONOMY
We will create 5 million new jobs within five years. Our government shall
reduce the cost of living by implementing price controls on essential goods.
We promise to establish a Ksh 100 billion youth enterprise fund.

HOUSING
We commit to building affordable housing units in every county. Our government
will ensure that young people can access low interest mortgages. We shall
introduce a national housing scheme for civil servants.
