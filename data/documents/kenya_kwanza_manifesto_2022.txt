KENYA KWANZA MANIFESTO 2022 - EXTRACT

AGRICULTURE
We will ensure that farmers receive subsidized fertilizer at Ksh 2,500 per bag.
Our government shall implement a nationwide irrigation program to support
small-scale farmers. We promise to establish agricultural processing zones
in every county.

HOUSING
We commit to building 500,000 affordable housing units across the country.
Our government will ensure that every Kenyan can own a home through
low-interest mortgage programs. We shall introduce a housing levy to fund
this initiative.

HEALTHCARE
We will ensure that NHIF covers all outpatient services. Our government
shall employ 100,000 community health workers. We promise to equip all
Level 4 hospitals with modern diagnostic equipment.

EDUCATION
We will implement a competency based curriculum fully. Our government shall
ensure that every classroom has a digital learning device. We promise to
hire 30,000 additional teachers.

ECONOMY
We will create jobs for the youth through tax incentives. Our government
shall reduce the cost of doing business to attract foreign investment.
We promise to establish a hustler fund for small businesses.
